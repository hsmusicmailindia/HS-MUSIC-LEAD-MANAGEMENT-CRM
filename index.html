📋 HS Music — Google Lead Conversion SOP Playbook

Target: 20% Baseline se 40% Conversion Rate (Preetamnagar, Prayagraj)

🚫 GOLDEN RULE: Call Par Fees Kabhi Mat Batao

Phone call ka goal admission close karna nahi, sirf 30-Minute Free Trial Class book karna hai. Har Google enquiry par 5 minute ke andar call back karein.

01. 2-Minute Calling Script (Lead Aane Ke 5 Minute Ke Andar)

  • Opening: "Hello [Name], main HS Music Preetamnagar se bol raha hoon. Aapne abhi [Course] classes ke liye enquiry ki thi—yeh aap khud ke liye dekh rahe hain ya apne bacche ke liye?"
  • Qualify: "Badhiya. Aapka main goal kya hai—hobby ke liye seekhna hai, stage ya YouTube ke liye, ya proper certification ke saath?"
  • Fee Objection Handle: "Fees aapke course aur batch level par depend karti hai. Lekin main recommend karunga ki bina teacher aur mahol dekhe aap decide na karein. Kal shaam 5 baje ya 6 baje hamara ek free 30-minute trial slot khali hai—kaunsa time aapke liye better rahega?"
  • Lock the Slot: "Done, maine aapka slot [Time] par book kar diya hai. Main abhi WhatsApp par center ki location aur confirmation bhej raha hoon. Kal milte hain!"

02. 30-Minute Free Trial Class & Desk Closing

  • 0–5 Min (Warm Welcome): Student aur parents ko comfortably bithayein, paani offer karein, favourite gaana/artist poochein.
  • 5–20 Min (Practical Win — No Theory): Teacher unhe usi favourite gaane ka 1 simple chord/melody bajwa kar dikhayein taaki pehle hi din win feel ho.
  • 20–30 Min (Desk Closing): Trial ke turant baad reception desk par 3-Month Quarterly Plan (10% discount ke saath) primary pitch karein. Monthly plan sirf secondary backup rakhein.

03. 7-Day Automated WhatsApp Follow-Up Plan

  • Trial Day (Subah 11 baje): Short confirmation WhatsApp bhejein taaki no-show kam ho.
  • Day 0 (Trial ke 2 ghante baad): Positive reinforcement & soft close ("is week se batch start karein?").
  • Day 2 (Shaam 5–6 baje): Social proof — 30-sec Student Reel ya Google Review screenshot bhejein.
  • Day 5 (Dopahar 12–1 baje): Urgency — "Batch mein sirf 2 seats bachi hain + complimentary studio jam session."
  • Day 7 (Shaam 6 baje): Break-up message taaki dead leads revive hon.
